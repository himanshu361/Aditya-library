📚 Current Curriculum Focus

The website is structured to keep separate CBSE and UP Board study tracks. For CBSE, the Academic Session 2026–27 curriculum is used as the current reference. For UP Board, the current UPMSP board-issued syllabus is used as the reference. NEP 2020/NCF-SE 2023 principles are reflected where applicable.

🔒 Notes & Content Privacy

Aditya Library study material is intended for registered students and personal educational use only.

  • Do not share, forward, copy, resell or redistribute downloaded notes or course content.
  • Students should not take screenshots, screen recordings or video recordings of protected course content.
  • Access may be limited to the registered student and authorised device/account.
  • Using the same registered phone number on another phone may require verification by Aditya Library.
  • Do not share your OTP, password, payment receipt or account credentials with another person.
Important: A normal website cannot technically guarantee that screenshots or screen recordings are impossible. These rules are intended to protect the centre's educational content and discourage unauthorised copying.
